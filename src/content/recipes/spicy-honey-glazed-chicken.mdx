---
name: Spicy Honey-Glazed Chicken Thighs
servings: about 4 servings
---

- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked chipotle pepper powder[^1]
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 6–8 chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on)[^2]
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

1. Preheat oven to 450&deg; F.
2. Combine cumin, pepper powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
3. Drizzle in the neutral oil,
4. mix to create paste.
5. Toss the chicken thighs in the spice paste until well coated.
6. Place well-coated chicken on an aluminum foil lined sheet pan.
7. Bake for 30 minutes (or until internal temp reaches 165&deg; F).

- &frac13; cup honey
- &frac13; cup hot sauce[^3]
- &frac14; cup butter
- 3 tbsp lemon juice (or the juice of one lemon)
- 1&frac12; tsp vinegar[^4]
- 1&frac12; cornstarch

1. While baking, in a medium sauce pan add honey and gently heat over medium heat.
2. Add butter and hot sauce, and mix until butter melts.
3. In a small bowl, mix together lemon juice, vinegar, cornstarch.
4. Drizzle the slurry into honey/hot-sauce mixture.
5. Stir until sauce thickens.
6. Remove from heat, until chicken is done.

{/* --- */}

1. Reduce oven to 325&deg; F.
1. Toss cooked chicken in hot-sauce mixture.
2. Return chicken to sheet pan.
3. Bake glazed chicken in oven until desired consistency (5–10 minutes)

[^1]: If chipotle pepper powder can be swapped for &half; tsp paprika and &half; tsp cayenne pepper.
[^2]: Boneless, skinless chicken thighs will also work.
[^3]: Any hot sauce you like will work, I like Crystal hot sauce.
[^4]: White vinegar, apple-cider vinegar, pickled jalapeño juice, whatever you've got.
