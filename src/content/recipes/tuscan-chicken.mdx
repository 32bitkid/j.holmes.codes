---
name: Tuscan Chicken
servings: about 4 servings
---

- 4 chicken thighs (skinless, boneless)[^1]
- Salt &amp; pepper to taste
- Neutral oil (just enough to almost coat the pan)

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
2. Preheat the oil in a pan over medium/medium-high heat.
3. Sauté the chicken until lightly browned, but not completely cooked through.
4. Remove from pan,
5. set aside until later.

- 1 white onion, diced[^2]
- 15-20 cherry tomatoes, halved[^3]
- 4oz–8oz white mushrooms, chopped[^4]
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)


{/* --- */}

1. Reduce heat to medium heat.
2. Into the same pan add onion, mushrooms, tomatoes, oregano, thyme, and red pepper flakes;
2. cook until onions turn translucent and start to soften.

{/* --- */}

- 4 gloves of garlic, minced
- 250 mL (1 cup) dry white wine[^5]
- 125 mL (&half; cup) whipping cream[^6]
- Handful of spinach (optional)
-
3. Add garlic.
4. Pour in wine and deglaze the bottom of the pan.
5. Reduce heat to medium-low.
6. Stir in cream until mixed;
7. add parcooked meat into sauce.
8. Simmer with lid on, on low heat, until sauce starts to thicken and meat finishes cooking.
9. Just before serving, add spinach, if desired.

[^1]: Substitution options: four chicken breast instead, or two turkey breasts, or even four pork chops.
[^2]: Optionally a medium shallot (or two small shallots) for the white onion.
[^3]: Two regular tomatoes, roughly chopped would also work (or even canned diced tomatoes, drained).
[^4]: Honestly, any mushrooms will do—or just leave them out if you don't have them/don't want them.
[^5]: If alcohol is not an option, replace white wine with chicken broth (or just water, in a pinch).
[^6]: Cream brings a lot to the sauce, but if unavailable sub in regular milk and add a knob (1–2 tbsp) of butter.
